Aircast
Wireless · HD · One Tap

Mirror your phone
to any TV.

Aircast is a free Android app that casts your phone screen to any smart TV. Samsung, LG, Sony, Roku, Fire TV, Chromecast. They all just work. No cables, no sign-up, no account.

▶ Get it on Google Play

What it does

  • One-tap screen mirroring to any smart TV on your Wi-Fi network
  • Works with Samsung, LG, Sony, Roku, Fire TV, Chromecast, Apple TV, Hisense, TCL, Vizio, and more
  • Available in 15 languages
  • Brand-specific setup walkthrough for the major TV makers
  • Free, ad-supported
