Privacy Policy for Aircast

Last updated: March 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Aircast ("the App") handles your information. Aircast is a screen mirroring application designed to connect your mobile device to displays on the same local network.

1. Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or private information from your device. All screen mirroring and content transmission occur strictly within your local Wi-Fi network.

2. Permissions Used

The App requires the following permissions to function:

  • INTERNET: To facilitate the local streaming protocol.
  • ACCESS_WIFI_STATE / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: To detect and connect to displays on your local network.
  • AD_ID: Used only for Play Store compliance and identifying device platform. No personal tracking is performed by the developer.

3. Screen Capture

The App uses system APIs (MediaProjection on Android and ReplayKit on iOS) to capture your screen for the purposes of mirroring. This data is processed in real-time and is never recorded, saved, or uploaded to any external server.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not share any data with third-party advertisers or analytics providers. There is no background tracking or profiling of user behavior.

5. Security

Because the App operates on your local network, the security of your connection depends on the security of your own Wi-Fi network.
